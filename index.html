Meet Lumi

Lumi is a small glowing spirit with a heart of light. Happy when you’re happy, quietly there when you’re not. Lumi never judges and never rushes you.

Your stories stay yours

  • Your journal is stored on your phone, encrypted. We don’t have a server that keeps it.
  • No account, no sign-up, no social feed.
  • Lock Feelow with Face ID, fingerprint or a PIN.
  • Export an encrypted backup whenever you like — you choose where it goes.
  • Ads are never shown while you write or record.
